## 2. QURBANI / UDHIYAH

### What Is Qurbani?
Qurbani (also called Udhiyah) is the Islamic practice of sacrificing an animal during Eid al-Adha, following the tradition of Prophet Ibrahim (AS). It is obligatory (wajib) on every eligible Muslim who meets the financial threshold (nisab).

### When Is Qurbani Performed?
Qurbani is performed during Eid al-Adha, on the 10th, 11th, or 12th of Dhul Hijjah (the last month of the Islamic calendar). The exact dates change each year based on the lunar calendar.

### How to Order
- **Online:** www.qurbani.com/qurbani

### Is Qurbani Zakat-Eligible?
No. Qurbani is a separate obligation and is NOT Zakat-eligible.

## 5. ZAKAT AL-FITR (FITRANA)

### What Is Zakat al-Fitr?
Zakat al-Fitr (also called Fitrana) is a charitable obligation on every Muslim at the end of Ramadan, before the Eid prayer. It purifies the fast and ensures the poor can celebrate Eid.

### Pricing
- **$10 per person**
- Required for **every Muslim household member**, including children, elderly, and dependents
- Example: Family of 4 = $40, Family of 7 = $70

### Key Details
- **Must be paid before Eid prayer**
- **Zakat al-Fitr is NOT the same as Zakat** -- it is a separate obligation

## 9. SADAQAH SACRIFICE

### What Is Sadaqah Sacrifice?
A Sadaqah sacrifice is a voluntary animal sacrifice performed for the sake of Allah. Unlike Qurbani (which is obligatory during Eid al-Adha), a Sadaqah sacrifice can be performed at any time of the year. It is recommended for seeking healing, protection, giving thanks, or fulfilling a vow.

### Pricing
| Option | Price | Impact |
|--------|-------|--------|
| 1 Sheep | $100 | Feeds a family of 6-8 people |
| 3 Sheep | $300 | Triple your reward |
| 5 Sheep | $500 | Feed an entire neighborhood |
| 10 Sheep | $1,000 | Transform a community |

### Key Details
- **Available year-round** (not limited to Eid)
- **Monthly sacrifice available** ($100/month and up)
- **Can be given on behalf of sick loved ones** -- the Prophet (PBUH) said: "Treat your sick by giving Sadaqah"
- **Can be given on behalf of deceased**
- **Zakat-eligible:** No
- **Tax-deductible:** Yes
- **Website:** www.qurbani.com/sadaqah-sacrifice

### Difference from Qurbani
Qurbani is obligatory and performed only during Eid al-Adha (10th-12th Dhul Hijjah). Sadaqah sacrifice is voluntary and can be done anytime throughout the year.
